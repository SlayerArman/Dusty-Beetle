# Dusty Beetle 
Your job is to dodge their hooves and try not to get turned into a tiny blue pancake.

> Dusty Beetle is a 2D arcade game I made using Godot 4.7. It's pretty simple to understand, but things get chaotic real quick when the horses start coming faster.

## How to Play?
Move around, dodge the incoming horses, and try to survive. That's pretty much it.
There are 20 campaign levels to complete, each with a timer. Beat a level to unlock the next one, and your progress saves automatically.
If you just want to see how long you can last, there's also an endless mode.
The longer you survive, the faster things get. Your beetle gets faster as well, so at least you've got a survival chance.

## Playable Link:
https://slayerarman.itch.io/dusty-beetle

## Controls
- Move: WASD or Arrow Keys
- Pause or resume: P
- Restart after dying: Space
